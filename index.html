About

PhD Spotlight is an afternoon where PhD students at the Department of Computer Science, University of Pisa, present their research through posters and short talks.

If you are a student curious about research, it's a chance to see what a PhD looks like from the inside, find ideas for your thesis and meet the people behind the projects. If you are a PhD student, it's the place to share your work, and to get to know the other groups of the Department if you have just started.

Program draft · times to be announced

  1. Keynote talks or lightning pitches Format to be confirmed: either 3–4 talks of 10–15 minutes covering the Department's main research areas, or short pitches to give space to as many posters as possible, niche topics included.
  2. Poster session Walk around the posters and talk directly with the PhD students.
  3. Refreshments To keep the conversations going.
